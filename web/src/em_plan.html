How deep each set-up sees

rules of thumb

TEM: Spies (1989), 0.55 (M ρ / η)1/5, capped by the diffusion depth at the 10 ms gate. AMT: Bostick depth 356 √(ρ / f). The vertical line is the host resistivity chosen above.

Expected resistivities

typical ranges

Basement host rocks are resistive, so good EM targets are conductors (sulphides, graphite, altered shear zones) at the contacts of the gravity and magnetic bodies.

Stations

1 · Reference line R1

Measure first over the quiet area: it gives the background resistivity and the noise level that set how deep the other lines can see.

2 · TEM lines, priority 1

Central-loop soundings, square loop of the listed side, ≥ 10 A, gates to ≥ 10 ms; stack until the late gates are stable. Lines may shift up to ~300 m to follow tracks; keep them across strike.

3 · Deep lines and AMT

400 m loops plus tensor AMT (10 Hz–10 kHz, 50 m dipoles, remote reference) on the deep targets, then the regional AMT traverse P1 along A–B.

Depth caveat

Target depths come from a potential-field inversion and are uncertain by a few hundred metres; compact and smooth models fit the data equally well. Saline wadi fill near the coast shortens TEM penetration.
